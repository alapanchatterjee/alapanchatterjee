## Alapan Chatterjee

Final-year B.Tech Aerospace Engineering student at SRM Institute of Science and Technology, Chennai (2023–2027). From December 2026 I will carry out my bachelor's thesis at ISRO's Liquid Propulsion Systems Centre, Bengaluru.

### Selected work

- **ASTRA-NEO**: preliminary design of a sample-return mission to the near-Earth asteroid 1999 AO10 on LVM3, verified in GMAT and checked with a 200,000-sample Monte Carlo analysis.
- **Hybrid-rocket additives**: a NASA CEA study of 72 additives for paraffin with high-test peroxide, and a diagnostic model of where each gain in specific impulse comes from. Best Paper Award at ICAME 2026; journal paper under review.
- **Mars hover propulsion** at Beyond Space Technologies: an electric ducted fan sized with OpenFOAM CFD, and a feasibility model of ionic propulsion.
- **Lamb-wave damage detection under load** at NIT Meghalaya: a COMSOL model with Python processing, in [lamb-wave-trm-load-invariance](https://github.com/alapanchatterjee/lamb-wave-trm-load-invariance).
- **Aero-engine oil-system heat** at HAL, a **1U CubeSat prototype** at STAR, fixed-wing UAVs with SRM's student team, and propulsion hardware.

Tools: GMAT · SPICE · Python · MATLAB · NASA CEA · OpenFOAM · COMSOL · XFLR5 · SolidWorks · Fusion 360
